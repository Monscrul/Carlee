How to play

  1. 1

    Search a car

    Type any make, model, or chassis code (like E30 or 992) and pick one from the list. Each result shows its debut year, engine, and horsepower underneath the name.

  2. 2

    Submit your guess

    Every guess is compared against the secret car, attribute by attribute.

  3. 3

    Read the colors

    Green means an exact match. Yellow means no match. Blue arrows on Year and HP point toward the answer.

  4. 4

    Solve it in six

    Narrow it down and name today’s car before your guesses run out. Same model, different generation is a different car. Year is that generation’s first year, not every trim year.
