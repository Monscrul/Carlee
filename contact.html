What to include

Helpful messages often include:

  • Feedback on the Daily puzzle or on Unlimited mode
  • Questions about how the game works
  • Bug reports (what you did, what you expected, and what happened)
  • Streak or statistics problems, for example a streak that reset unexpectedly
  • Corrections to car data (make, model, year, specs, and so on)
  • Feature ideas, privacy, or other inquiries

Streaks and stats are stored in your own browser rather than in an account, so there is no record to look up on our side. For stats issues, the most useful thing you can send is the steps to reproduce it, plus your browser and device.

There is no contact form on this site yet; Carlee does not currently run a backend for form submissions, so email is the contact method for now.
