What Carlee is

Carlee is a free browser-based car-guessing game. The main mode is Daily: one mystery car per UTC calendar day, the same car for every player, solved in up to 6 guesses. An optional Unlimited mode lets you play random cars as often as you like and does not count toward streaks or win rate.

You identify the car by searching and submitting guesses. Feedback compares attributes such as make, country, drivetrain, body style, horsepower, year, and engine.

Carlee currently does not require accounts and does not provide a contact form backend. The production site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to measure visits and performance. It does not currently include advertising. Features may change over time.

Streaks and statistics

Streaks, win rate, and other statistics are informal records stored in your own browser on the device you play on. They are not an account, not a competitive ranking, and not backed up by us.

Because this data lives only in your browser, it can be lost if you clear site data, use private browsing, switch browsers or devices, or if your browser evicts stored data. We cannot restore, transfer, or verify a lost streak, and we may reset or change how statistics work as the game evolves.
