What Carlee is

Carlee is a free browser-based car-guessing game. Each round, a mystery car is chosen from a catalog, and you try to identify it by searching and submitting guesses. Feedback compares attributes such as make, country, drivetrain, body style, horsepower, year, and engine. You have a limited number of guesses per game.
