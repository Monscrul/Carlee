What Carlee is

Carlee is a free browser-based car-guessing game. The main mode is Daily: one mystery car per UTC calendar day, the same car for every player, solved in up to 6 guesses. An optional Unlimited mode lets you play random cars as often as you like and does not count toward streaks or win rate.

You identify the car by searching and submitting guesses. Feedback compares attributes such as make, country, drivetrain, body style, horsepower, year, and engine.

Accounts are optional. You may play without signing in, or sign in with Google or email and password to sync Daily progress and statistics across devices. Authentication and synced data are provided through Supabase. The production site also uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to measure visits and performance. It does not currently include advertising. Features may change over time.

Streaks and statistics

Streaks, win rate, and other Daily statistics are informal records for your own play. They are not a competitive ranking and are not prizes.

Without an account, statistics and Daily progress are stored only in your browser on that device. They can be lost if you clear site data, use private browsing, switch browsers or devices, or if your browser removes stored data.

With an account, Daily statistics and today’s Daily game progress may sync to your account so you can continue on another device. Unlimited mode is not synced. Signing out clears local game data on that device; your account data remains until you request deletion or the account is otherwise removed. We may reset or change how statistics work as the game evolves, and we cannot guarantee recovery of every local-only streak that was never synced.
