About Carlee

A small car-guessing game built for people who like cars — and a good puzzle.

What is Carlee?

Carlee is a browser-based guessing game. Each round, a mystery car is chosen from the catalog, and you try to figure out which one it is by making guesses and reading the feedback.

How the game works

Search for a make or model, submit a guess, and compare attributes like make, country, drivetrain, body style, horsepower, year, and engine. Matching fields turn correct; year and horsepower also show whether you need to go higher or lower. You have a limited number of guesses to find the right car.

Why Carlee was created

Carlee started as a simple personal project: a clean, focused place to play a car-themed guessing game without clutter. The goal is to keep the experience fast, readable, and fun whether you know cars well or are just learning the badges and specs.

What makes Carlee different

Carlee keeps the focus on the puzzle itself — search, guess, compare, repeat. There is no account signup required to play, and the interface stays intentionally simple so the cars and the feedback stay front and center.

About the project

Carlee is an independent side project. Features may grow over time (for example, richer statistics), but the core idea remains the same: one mystery car, a handful of guesses, and a catalog worth exploring.
